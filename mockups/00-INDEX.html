01 — Bloomberg Terminal

Dense, information-heavy layout inspired by Bloomberg terminals. Two-column grid with markets on the left, weather/traffic/monitoring on the right, news spanning the bottom.
2-column dense classic green-on-black

02 — Mission Control

Three-column layout with hero numbers in the center. Large BTC price and weather temperature as focal points. Sidebar panels for detail, center for at-a-glance info.
3-column hero-numbers balanced green-on-black

03 — Hacker Minimal

Pure text, no boxes or borders. Everything looks like terminal output. Giant clock, clean spacing, maximum readability from a distance. The most "walking past" friendly.
pure-text minimal large-type green-on-black

04 — Amber Retro

Amber/orange phosphor color scheme like vintage IBM terminals. Four-quadrant layout with heavy borders. Distinct retro feel that's different from the green-screen look.
4-quadrant amber vintage heavy-borders

05 — Cyberpunk Neon

Multi-color neon aesthetic — cyan, pink, green on dark purple. Corner accents, gradient fills, neon glow effects. The most visually striking option. Has an expansion area for future panels.
multi-color neon-glow cyberpunk expandable

06 — Synthwave 80s

Pink/cyan neon on deep purple. Orbitron font for that 80s Outrun feel. Sunset gradient strip, perspective grid floor. Like driving a DeLorean through your data.
80s-retro pink-cyan orbitron-font gradient-heavy

07 — Tactical HUD

Military ops center aesthetic. OD green, corner brackets, compass rose with wind direction, solar timeline, 7-day temp bars. Feels like you're commanding a base. "SIGINT Feed" instead of "News".
military 3-column compass OD-green

08 — Frosted Glass

Modern glassmorphism on a deep purple gradient. Rounded cards with backdrop blur, pill badges for RSI states, Inter + JetBrains Mono fonts. Clean, premium, almost iOS-like. The anti-terminal.
glassmorphism modern rounded purple-gradient

09 — Red Alert / DEFCON

Red-on-black emergency aesthetic. DEFCON indicator that reacts to market signals, alert boxes for overbought/down sites, warning stripes. Dramatic and attention-grabbing. Blinking indicators for down sites.
red-theme alerts DEFCON dramatic

10 — Digital Broadsheet

Light background like a newspaper. Playfair Display serif headlines, article excerpts, category tags. Markets in a sidebar column. The most readable from any distance — and totally unexpected for a Pi dashboard.
light-theme serif-fonts newspaper 4-column
How to use:
1. Open each mockup to compare layouts
2. Pick your favorite (or mix elements from multiple)
3. The final dashboard will be pure HTML/CSS/JS — no Canvas, maximum readability
4. All content is configurable via hub.config.md — see the config file in the project root

Note: These are static HTML mockups with hardcoded data — the final version will fetch live data from APIs.
